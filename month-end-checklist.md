# Month-end checklist: veterinary practice

Work top to bottom. Deposits come first because the revenue split, the fees
and the pharmacy numbers all start from them.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Bank and card statements for the full month, as PDFs
- [ ] Card processor monthly statement (gross volume, fees, deposits)
- [ ] Practice management sales summary for the month, split into services
      and pharmacy and food
- [ ] Patient financing payout reports
- [ ] Drug distributor, reference lab and cremation provider invoices or
      statements
- [ ] Equipment loan and lease statements
- [ ] Payroll report for every pay run in the month

## 2. Gross up the deposits
- [ ] Card deposits grossed back up to the sales they came from; processor fees
      booked as merchant fees
- [ ] Each financing payout booked at the full bill as revenue, with the
      provider fee as its own expense
- [ ] Revenue in the books agrees with the practice management summary for
      the month

## 3. Keep services and pharmacy apart
- [ ] Exams, surgery, dentistry and diagnostics in exam and service revenue
- [ ] Medications, preventives and prescription diets in pharmacy and food
      sales
- [ ] Pharmacy and food sales compared with last month

## 4. Cost lines
- [ ] Distributor invoices in drugs and medical supplies
- [ ] Reference lab and imaging read fees in outside lab and imaging fees
- [ ] Cremation provider invoices in cremation and aftercare
- [ ] Each vendor statement agrees with what the books owe that vendor

## 5. Client deposits
- [ ] Deposits taken before surgery or boarding held as client deposits
- [ ] Deposits used this month moved into revenue

## 6. Equipment loans
- [ ] Each loan payment split: interest to interest expense, principal against
      the loan
- [ ] Loan balance in the books equals the lender's statement
- [ ] Monthly depreciation posted for the equipment

## 7. Payroll from the report
- [ ] Gross wages, employer taxes and withholdings booked from the payroll
      report, not the net pay leaving the bank

## 8. Reconcile and review
- [ ] Every bank and card account ties to its printed closing balance
- [ ] Profit and loss compared with last month; any big move explained
- [ ] Uncategorized at zero

## 9. Lock and send
- [ ] Month locked so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Reports sent to the practice owner, with open questions listed
